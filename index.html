Our facilities

Specialist departments, centred on you.

From accurate diagnosis to dedicated treatment, our departments work together to support your health.

Laboratory

Equipped with the latest diagnostic tools, ensuring accurate and timely results, from routine blood tests to complex genetic analysis.

Dialysis Unit

With 12 stations, we offer a world of specialised renal care, with a dedicated team committed to preserving your kidney health.

Ultrasound

Top-of-the-line ultrasound technology for clear, detailed images for precise diagnosis and treatment planning.

Delivery Unit

Equipped with a baby resuscitator for the unique emergency of moving a new life into the world.

Emergency Unit

State-of-the-art emergency unit with 2 beds, oxygen outlets, defibrillator and patient monitors, ready to handle emergencies that may come.

Surgical Unit

In our surgical theatres, precision and sterility are paramount, backed by advanced equipment for minimal invasiveness.

High Dependency Unit

We monitor the progress of critical patients with patient monitors, defibrillators and oxygen outlets.

Intensive Care Unit

Equipped with patient monitors, defibrillators and oxygen outlets. We care for patients who need intensive care.

Private Wards

A 40-bed facility across private rooms, semi-private rooms, VIP rooms, male ward, female ward and maternity ward. 24-hour in-patient and out-patient care.

A lasting community impact

Surgical Mission at Lana Hospital

The just concluded Surgical Mission in Sapele leaves a mark of love and compassion in the community as families continue to express their gratitude to God and to Mission Africa in Nigeria, the ISM (International Surgical Mission) and all partners home and abroad. We are here to continue to deliver the best medical care the world has to offer right here in Sapele.
